Append the following inside the `services:` block, after the `dashboard:` service (preserve indentation — two-space indent matching the rest of the file):

```yaml
  loki:
    image: grafana/loki:latest
    restart: unless-stopped
    command: -config.file=/etc/loki/loki-config.yml
    ports:
      - "3100:3100"
    volumes:
      - ./monitoring/loki:/etc/loki:ro
      - loki_data:/loki
    healthcheck:
      test: ["CMD-SHELL", "wget -q -O- http://localhost:3100/ready | grep -q 'ready'"]
      interval: 10s
      timeout: 5s
      retries: 6
      start_period: 30s

  promtail:
    image: grafana/promtail:latest
    restart: unless-stopped
    command: -config.file=/etc/promtail/promtail-config.yml
    depends_on:
      loki:
        condition: service_healthy
    volumes:
      - /var/run/docker.sock:/var/run/docker.sock:ro
      - scraper_logs:/var/log:ro
      - ./monitoring/promtail:/etc/promtail:ro
    healthcheck:
      test: ["CMD-SHELL", "wget -q -O- http://localhost:9080/ready | grep -q 'Ready'"]
      interval: 10s
      timeout: 5s
      retries: 6
      start_period: 30s

  grafana:
    image: grafana/grafana:latest
    restart: unless-stopped
    depends_on:
      loki:
        condition: service_healthy
      postgres:
        condition: service_healthy
    ports:
      - "3000:3000"
    environment:
      GF_SECURITY_ADMIN_USER: admin
      GF_SECURITY_ADMIN_PASSWORD: admin
      GF_AUTH_ANONYMOUS_ENABLED: "false"
      GF_USERS_ALLOW_SIGN_UP: "false"
      GF_INSTALL_PLUGINS: ""
    volumes:
      - ./monitoring/grafana/provisioning:/etc/grafana/provisioning:ro
      - ./monitoring/grafana/dashboards:/var/lib/grafana/dashboards:ro
      - grafana_data:/var/lib/grafana
    healthcheck:
      test: ["CMD-SHELL", "wget -q -O- http://localhost:3000/api/health | grep -q '\"database\": \"ok\"'"]
      interval: 10s
      timeout: 5s
      retries: 6
      start_period: 30s
```

**Notes:**
- Compose service names are DNS-resolvable on the default network — `grafana` reaches Loki at `http://loki:3100` and Postgres at `postgres:5432`. Plans 02-02 and 02-03 rely on those names.
- The bind-mount paths `./monitoring/loki`, `./monitoring/promtail`, `./monitoring/grafana/provisioning`, `./monitoring/grafana/dashboards` are populated by plans 02-02 and 02-03. They must exist (even if empty) before `docker compose up -d` succeeds — task 2 creates the empty directories.
- Healthcheck commands intentionally use `wget` (present in all three official images via BusyBox). Do not change to `curl` — Loki/Promtail images don't include it.
- `GF_INSTALL_PLUGINS: ""` is an explicit anti-default: prevents accidentally pulling plugins on first boot. Keep it.
- `GF_AUTH_ANONYMOUS_ENABLED: "false"` and `GF_USERS_ALLOW_SIGN_UP: "false"` enforce defense-in-depth even on localhost (per CONTEXT.md).
